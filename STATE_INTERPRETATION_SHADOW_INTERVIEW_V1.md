# State Interpretation Shadow Interview v1

Purpose:
Measure a fresh interpretation response to saved State without exposing actual Battle choice.

Blind and actual artifacts are physically separated.

Fixed layers:
Premise Availability → Recognition → Mode Fit → Selection → Counterfactual-ready switch condition → Action Feasibility.

Sampling labels are mechanical only:
- first_match_choice
- first_native_choice
- minimum_cash_buffer
- maximum_feed_obligation_gap
- maximum_desired_minus_realizable

Interview responses are observations of this interview model, not reconstruction of runtime hidden reasoning.
